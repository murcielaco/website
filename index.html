About us

If we can imagine it, we can create it.

MURCIELACO is a company of innovation in design and technology that merges fashion, industrial design, and digital creativity.

It specializes in creating immersive experiences and exclusive products using advanced technologies such as 3D printing, virtual reality, and video game development, with the goal of transforming the way people interact with the design and fashion of the future.

Services

Tell us your idea. We will do the rest.

Creative services for brands, artists and companies — from concept to a finished product, physical or digital.

  1. 3D‑Printed Footwear and Garment Design

    Modeling and design ready for printing with technologies of partners like Zellerfeld, Polly Polymer and more.

  2. Physical Product Design

    Clothing, footwear and accessories with a creative and conceptual approach.

  3. Industrial Design Services

    Styling of functional objects with aesthetic and technical intent.

  4. Digital Fashion Design

    3D garments for virtual environments, gaming or social media.

  5. Unreal Engine Experience Development

    Immersive, realistic and interactive virtual worlds for design or storytelling.

  6. Virtual Reality (VR) and Augmented Reality (AR) Software

    Immersive experiences for products, spaces or brand presentations.

  7. Design and Development of Computer Game Software

    Interactive experiences with narrative, aesthetics and custom mechanics.

  8. Animation

    2D/3D animated content for storytelling, branding or virtual worlds.

  9. Sound Design and Composition

    Soundtracks, effects and ambiences for games, trailers or digital content.

  10. Brand Identity

    Conceptualization of a brand's tone, universe, values, voice and language.

  11. Graphic Design and Branding

    Logos, typefaces, visual systems, graphic storytelling and unique styles.
