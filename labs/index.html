MURCIELACO LABS

Where ideas become tangible products.

MURCIELACO LABS is the core of technological innovation and product development for the brand. Here, we focus on researching and developing new printing and manufacturing techniques, including the design and conceptualization of advanced production equipment.

LABS also plays a crucial role in industrial design, where new products such as 3D-printed garments are created, tested, and evaluated to ensure they meet the quality and innovative design standards that characterize MURCIELACO. This lab is the epicenter where ideas are transformed into tangible prototypes and final products ready for the market.

ObjectiveThe objective of MURCIELACO LABS is to lead the forefront of innovation in the fashion industry and beyond, developing pioneering solutions and cutting-edge products that define the future of fashion and design. Our mission is to transform ambitious ideas into tangible products that enhance the quality and sustainability of our products and processes.

Introducing LAB‑01

A 3D resin printer made for footwear.

LAB-01 is a 3D resin printer exclusively designed for footwear printing, conceived as an innovative solution to optimize manufacturing in the footwear design industry. This conceptual model is developed with a focus on efficiency, offering advanced capabilities for both custom and mass production of footwear.

3D Resin Printer LAB‑01

Its resin technology allows for greater precision and detail in each print, making it ideal for reproducing complex textures and ergonomic shapes that ensure both aesthetic design and functional performance in footwear. LAB-01 features an optimized printing system capable of handling flexible and durable materials, which is key to producing high-quality shoes, particularly fully 3D-printed footwear.

Designed with an intuitive interface, this printer is equipped with custom software that allows for 3D previews of models before printing, and its high-speed printing capability significantly reduces production times. Additionally, its modular design facilitates easy maintenance and component upgrades.

Fashion for the space

Introducing X‑PACE.

First 3D-printed footwear designed for space missions.

At MURCIELACO, we are pioneers in 3D-printed footwear design, standing out for merging cutting-edge technology with sophisticated design. We are the first 3D footwear manufacturer in Latin America, and in the past, we have launched models like the “SPIDER,” a 100% recyclable shoe made from a single piece.

Now, we are taking a step further with the development of X-PACE, the first 3D-printed footwear specifically designed for space missions.

Concept and design

Inspired by the Opportunity rover.

The X-PACE for SpaceX is the first 3D-printed footwear designed for space missions, created to provide comfort and style without the need for pressurization inside spacecraft and space stations. Its minimalist, laceless design makes it easy to put on and take off, ideal for the space environment where every second counts and simplicity is key.

Inspired by the interplanetary future envisioned by SpaceX, its design takes cues from the Opportunity rover, with its wheel system and grip patterns reinterpreted in the sole of the X-PACE to offer optimal support on various surfaces. Its organic and futuristic appearance embodies the spirit of MURCIELACO, merging aesthetics and functionality with the sophistication of modern spacecraft elements, resulting in a product that is not only functional but also reflects the aesthetics of an advanced space age.

Advantages of X‑PACE footwear

Built for microgravity.

01

Cordless design

Being made from a single piece without laces, zippers, or Velcro, the X-PACE are easy to put on and take off, optimizing time and reducing the complexity of maneuvering in microgravity environments while avoiding any unnecessary handling.

02

Sustainable 3D manufacturing

Footwear produced with a low environmental impact, demonstrating our strong commitment to sustainability even in a high-tech environment.

03

Production in space

Being 3D-printed footwear, there is the possibility of manufacturing them on board, reducing dependence on supplies sent from Earth.

Comfort and convenience

Organic parametric design.

Comfort and ergonomics in space

Designed to provide continuous support inside the spacecraft, with the comfort of crew members on long-duration missions in mind, the X-PACE feature an ergonomic design that adapts to the shape of the crew member’s foot and a parametric organic sole padding that offers even weight distribution while maintaining the footwear’s flexibility and durability.

Optimized functionality

Optimized grip.

The sole of the X-PACE is designed to maximize traction on both smooth and uneven surfaces, inspired by the traction of the Opportunity rover on Mars, ensuring stability and support in various scenarios.

Fashion of the future

Conceptual designs for the space future.

For the conceptual development of the X-PACE clothing line, we designed space suits that perfectly complement the aesthetic of the footwear. These conceptual designs are heavily inspired by science fiction scenarios, envisioning a future where multi-planetary humanity overcomes the limitations of space life through technological advancements. This impact is directly reflected in the fusion of functionality and style.

Suits for space tourism

Concept and functionality.

Every aspect of the design of these suits was carefully considered to ensure that long-duration space travelers feel comfortable and protected. Their practicality and minimalist design prioritize ease of movement and protection against variable temperatures, while still maintaining a unique stylish touch that offers a more futuristic vision than conventional space clothing.

Our vision of an interplanetary future

At MURCIELACO, we share SpaceX’s vision of interplanetary societies, envisioning a future in space where fashion and technology coexist and converge in garments that have a direct impact on the quality of life of future societies. We foresee a world where 3D printing plays a fundamental role, transforming the way everyday objects are produced and distributed.

We envision a future where spacecraft will be equipped with 3D printers capable of manufacturing any type of garment or accessory needed, adapting to the circumstances faced by their crew. In case of an emergency, crew members will be able to print necessary objects—such as eyeglass frames, tools, or even footwear—directly on the ship from 3D files sent from Earth.

Our vision is to facilitate a safer and more comfortable exploration experience, where each crew member has access to the necessary tools and garments, produced immediately and sustainably. Fashion and design will no longer be just a matter of style, but also a vital tool to improve the quality of life in an interplanetary context.

A step towards the future

The first contact is through the feet.

Interestingly, humanity’s first contact with the surface of another planet is through the feet. When Neil Armstrong descended from the lunar module and stepped onto the Moon’s surface, his feet were not only touching unknown ground but also the future.

The feet are the first physical connection that humans have when exploring a new world. Each step represents a historic moment and an act of conquest, a declaration of our presence on that unfamiliar terrain. The footwear that astronauts wear holds unparalleled symbolic and practical significance: an essential tool for mobility and safety, and an extension of our desire to explore, adapt, and thrive in environments so foreign to those on Earth.

The first contact with the surface of Mars could be marked by footwear that not only protects and facilitates exploration but also reflects human vision and creativity — a work of art and technology that tells the story of how we arrived at that moment. This idea is precisely what inspires the design of MURCIELACO. X-PACE aims to be more than just footwear; it seeks to be a symbol of the human spirit in exploring new horizons.
